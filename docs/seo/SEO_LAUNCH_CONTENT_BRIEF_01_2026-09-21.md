# SEO Launch Content Brief 01 — AI Market Intelligence

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Status:** `SEO_SOURCE_BRIEF / NOT_PUBLISHED`  
**Owner:** `CAPITAL-AI-SEO`  
**Target canonical URL:** `https://capital-ai.online/`  
**Primary intent:** brand discovery + product investigation  
**Primary audience:** users evaluating an AI-assisted multi-asset market analysis platform

## User problem

A visitor needs to understand quickly what CAPITAL-AI does, how its AI/scoring is bounded, what markets it covers and why the platform is different from opaque or demo-heavy finance products.

## Core proposition

CAPITAL-AI combines market information, explainable AI-assisted scoring, research and learning in a multi-asset interface with explicit evidence and access boundaries.

## Proof boundaries

Allowed source-backed themes:

- multi-asset positioning across equities, crypto, forex, commodities and indices;
- explainable/transparent analysis and scoring language;
- research + learning surfaces;
- evidence-first/no-demo-data positioning;
- analysis and education, not personalized investment advice.

Do not claim without fresh evidence:

- investment performance or returns;
- predictive accuracy;
- user counts or customer outcomes;
- “real-time” latency guarantees;
- ranking/traffic/search visibility;
- provider coverage beyond verified product/runtime evidence.

1. What CAPITAL-AI combines.
2. Explainable scoring and evidence boundaries.
3. Multi-asset scope.
4. Research and learning.
5. How the public analysis entry works.
6. Trust/disclosure: no demo data, no personalized investment advice.
7. Contextual links to:
   - `/universe`;
   - `/learning-platform`;
   - legal/privacy surfaces where relevant.
