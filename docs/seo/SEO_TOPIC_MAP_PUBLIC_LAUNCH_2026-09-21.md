## Internal-link priorities

1. `/` → `/universe` with descriptive anchor tied to multi-asset discovery/intelligence.
2. `/` → `/learning-platform` already exists; preserve and contextualize where useful.
3. `/universe` → `/learning-platform` exists; preserve vocabulary/support relationship.
4. Future public methodology content should link back to the exact product surface it explains and to supporting learning content where useful.

## Content guardrails

- people-first, original and product-grounded;
- no scaled keyword-variant pages;
- no fabricated rankings, returns, reach or user outcomes;
- no “best investment”/personalized recommendation framing without separately authorized domain architecture;
- no duplicate doorway pages per asset or keyword;
- structured data only for content actually visible on the target page;
- each new content asset requires source/provenance, reviewer, canonical URL, internal-link plan, measurement key and refresh date.

## Data needed for quantitative reprioritization

Once a real read surface is connected:

- GSC query impressions/clicks/CTR;
- GSC landing-page performance;
- Search Console index/inspection state;
- GA4 organic acquisition and landing engagement;
- actual signup/login funnel contribution;
- real Social referral performance after publication.

Until then, this map is a qualitative product/search-intent baseline.
