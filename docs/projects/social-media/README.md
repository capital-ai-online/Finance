## Scope

CAPITAL-AI-SOCIAL coordinates Social Media channel planning, distribution workflows, content-format mappings, platform-specific execution planning, handoffs, reporting and evidence for approved content.

It owns **no productive `PVC-*` stage** and does not create an implicit `PVC-19`. Content generation, SEO/discoverability, Frontend implementation, Governance/publishing approval, Compliance assessment and protected provider/platform operations remain with their canonical owners.

This project folder is organizational navigation only. It does not replace `docs/social-media/CAPITAL-AI-SOCIAL/**`, accepted content/publishing contracts, Marketing Agent governance or external platform mutation controls.

## Ownership boundary

Social owns:

- Social channel/distribution planning;
- platform-specific content-format planning and mappings;
- distribution work-package coordination;
- content-contract consumption within delegated scope;
- Social handoffs and returned execution evidence;
- non-authorizing reporting and continuous improvement inputs.

Social does not own:

- autonomous publication approval;
- external account/credential/IAM authority;
- provider/platform mutation authority merely by owning distribution planning;
- SEO/discoverability authority outside the SEO project;
- Frontend/product implementation;
- Governance/control policy;
- legal/compliance assessment;
- productive data/scoring/business logic;
- a new productive PVC stage.

## Cross-project execution model

Social consumes approved content/contracts and coordinates distribution. Where external publication or provider mutation is protected, execution remains behind the applicable Human/Owner, Operations, credential and platform gates. Social cannot turn a planned post into an authorized publication by itself.

SEO/marketing discoverability work is routed to `CAPITAL-AI-SEO`; public UI/product work to `CAPITAL-AI-FE`; protected provider/runtime work to `CAPITAL-AI-OPS`; governance/publishing-control work to `CAPITAL-AI-GOV`; compliance assessment to `CAPITAL-AI-COMP`.

## Validation / Definition of Done

The Social project surface remains valid when:

1. `docs/projects/social-media/` remains a non-authorizing navigation layer;
2. CAPITAL-AI-SOCIAL owns no productive `PVC-*` stage and no implicit `PVC-19` exists;
3. existing Social roadmap/contracts/mappings/handoffs remain canonical detail sources;
4. publication, credentials and external platform mutation remain behind existing approval/control gates;
5. SEO/Frontend/OPS/GOV/COMP work remains routed to those owners;
6. Social reports/evidence remain traceable to approved contracts and channel execution;
7. merged/closed Social work claims are terminalized and cannot remain active parallel writers.

## Non-goals

No duplicate Social architecture, no project-folder-driven relocation of the existing Social domain, no autonomous external publishing, no credential/platform authority, no implicit `PVC-19`, no productive PVC allocation and no replacement of SEO, Governance, Compliance, Frontend or Operations ownership.
